    Honesty section

    When you should hire someone else.

    Four situations where we're the wrong call. We'd rather say it here than take your deposit and disappoint you in week three.

    01

    You need it live tomorrow

    The fastest honest launch we do is about a week, and that's a small site with content ready to go. Anyone promising you a custom site by tomorrow is handing you a template with your logo on it.

    What to do instead Put up a one-page holding site yourself this week, then build the real thing properly behind it. We'll happily tell you how on the call, free.

    02

    Your budget is under ₹5,000

    Below that a page builder genuinely is the better answer, and we'll tell you which one on the call. Our Starter build starts at ₹7,999 — under five we'd be selling you something thin and taking the money.

    What to do instead Start on a builder, get the enquiries flowing, and come back when the site is costing you more in lost leads than a rebuild would cost outright.

    03

    You want your builder site maintained

    We don't patch other people's page-builder installs — the plugin stack is the problem, and monthly fixes just rent you the same fault. We'll quote a rebuild instead, or leave it alone.

    What to do instead Keep the maintenance with whoever built it, and ask us only when you're ready to stop paying monthly for the same recurring fault.

    04

    You want a guaranteed #1 ranking

    Nobody can sell that honestly, and the people who do are selling something else. We build sites that are fast, structured and indexable. Where you land is Google's call.

    What to do instead Hire an SEO specialist on a retainer for the ranking work — and hold them to reported numbers. We'll make sure the site they're working on isn't the problem.
