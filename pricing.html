How paying works

Two payments. No surprises between them.

The split below is what we usually do. It's negotiable, and for a first-time business or an early-stage startup we'll happily restructure it around your cash flow rather than ours.

Milestone 01 50%

To start the work

Paid after the day-zero call, once you've got the written scope note with a fixed price and a date on it. Nothing is charged before that — the call and the scope note are free whether or not you go ahead.

Milestone 02 50%

On launch day

Paid when the site is live on your domain and the handover is done — not when we say it's finished, but when you've seen it working. The free-fix window starts the same day.

What you are never billed for

  • The first call, and the written scope note that comes out of it
  • Both rounds of design revisions before the build starts
  • Anything broken inside the 30 or 60 day free-fix window
  • Transferring the code, domain and accounts into your name
  • Theme, template or plugin licences — there aren't any to renew
  • A monthly fee to us for keeping your own site online
