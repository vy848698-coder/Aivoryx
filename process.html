Phase 01 · Talk Day 00

The twenty-minute call

What we do

Ask about your business, your customers, and the single thing this site has to make happen. No slide deck, no sales script — we're working out whether we're the right people for it, and we'll say so if we aren't.

What you do 20 min · free

Twenty minutes on the phone or WhatsApp. Bring the question your worst customer always asks, not a written brief.

Yours by tonight
  • A written scope note with a fixed price and a date
Phase 02 · Design Day 01

Sitemap and content audit

What we do

Map every page the site actually needs and delete the ones it doesn't, then write down precisely which words and pictures we need from you — and the day we need each one.

What you do 30 min

Send whatever already exists: logo files, photos, old brochures, your current site. Messy is fine. Missing is the problem.

Yours by tonight
  • A page-by-page sitemap
  • A content checklist with a date against every item
Phase 02 · Design Day 02

Direction: type, colour, grid

What we do

Set the typography, colour and spacing rules before a single page is drawn. It's the difference between a site that's designed and one that's decorated page by page until nothing matches.

What you do Nothing

Today is entirely on us. You'll see the result tomorrow, applied to a real page rather than a mood board.

Yours by tonight
  • The visual direction, written down and reusable
Phase 02 · Design Day 03

The real homepage design

What we do

A complete homepage — your words, your images, final spacing. Not a grey wireframe you're asked to imagine in colour, and not a stock template with your logo dropped into the corner.

What you do 20 min

React honestly. Blunt feedback today costs twenty minutes; polite feedback today costs us both a week later on.

Yours by tonight
  • The finished homepage design
Phase 02 · Design Day 04

Revisions, then every other page

What we do

Apply your feedback — two full rounds are included — then template every remaining page off the signed-off homepage so the whole site stays consistent.

What you do 15 min

Sign it off, or tell us what's still wrong. This is the last cheap moment to change your mind: after today, changes cost days instead of hours.

Yours by tonight
  • A signed-off homepage design
  • A layout template for every inner page

Check our maths against your real numbers. You know your margins; we don't.

Your real words and pictures

Real phones, not a resized window

What we do

Test on actual Android and iOS handsets, on mobile data, at the sizes your customers really hold — including the cheap phone with the small screen that a browser emulator never quite imitates.

What you do 10 min

Speed and accessibility pass

What we do

Trim images, fonts and scripts until the site clears Core Web Vitals, then check contrast, keyboard navigation and screen-reader labels. On an Indian mobile network, one second of load time is the gap between an enquiry and a bounce.

What you do Nothing

Yours by tonight
  • A Core Web Vitals and accessibility report

    The awkward questions

    What happens when it doesn't go to plan.

    Every build hits at least one of these. Here's what we actually do — written down now, so nobody has to negotiate it in week three.

    01What if I don't like the design?

    Two full rounds of revisions sit inside day four, before a single line of production code exists. That ordering is deliberate: redrawing a design costs hours, rebuilding a coded site costs days. If two rounds haven't got us there, it usually means we've misread the business rather than the brief — and we'll say that out loud instead of quietly billing a third round.

    02What if my content isn't ready on day nine?

    The build reaches day nine and waits there. You'll hear from us the same day, not a fortnight later when the launch date has quietly moved. Your slot stays open for two weeks while you catch up, and we'll happily shape rough notes into copy — what we won't do is invent facts about a business we don't run.

    03Is fifteen days actually realistic?

    For a full Growth build, yes — that's the honest average, not a best case with the wind behind it. A five-page Starter site is closer to seven days. Web apps and mobile apps run four to ten weeks and are quoted per scope. When a project does slip, it is almost never the code: it is content and feedback, which is exactly why both have named days above.

    04Can I see it before it's finished?

    From day five, on a private preview link that rebuilds every time we push a change. You watch the site appear rather than waiting for a reveal, and you can send the link to a partner or a customer for a second opinion whenever you like.

    05What if I want changes after launch?

    Anything broken is fixed free for 30 days on a Starter build and 60 days on Growth. New work after that is an hourly rate or a small monthly retainer, whichever is cheaper for you. And because the code and every account are already in your name, you are equally free to hand it to somebody else — there is no lock-in to argue your way out of.

    06Do I have to pay everything up front?

    No. Part payment starts the work, the balance is due on launch. For early-stage startups and first-time local businesses the split is negotiable — say so on the day-zero call and we'll structure it around your cash flow rather than ours.
