MarketIQ Infotech

01The problem

A marketing agency gets plenty of enquiries. Most of them are worth nothing. Someone fills a contact form with "interested in your services", and the team burns a phone call finding out the person wanted a ₹3,000 logo.

The old site did what most agency sites do: name, email, message, send. Every lead arrived identical, so every lead had to be chased identically.

02The call we made

Ask the two uncomfortable questions on the form, not on the call. Which service, and what monthly budget. Yes, it costs you some submissions. That is the point — the ones you lose were never going to buy.

Then route the answers straight into WhatsApp rather than an inbox nobody opens on a Sunday. The message arrives pre-written, with the budget already in it, on the device the owner actually carries.

03What we shipped
  • Seven pages — home, services, courses, careers, about, contact, blog
  • Qualifying enquiry form: service + monthly budget captured up front
  • WhatsApp routing, so leads land on a phone, not in an inbox
  • Animated counters on the results section
  • Dedicated courses and careers pages with their own enquiry paths
  • Google Maps embed for the Bhubaneswar office

01The problem

Nobody buys solar because a website said solar is good. They buy it when they can see their own number — what it costs, how long until it pays back, what they keep afterwards.

Before this, that answer lived inside a salesperson's head. Every enquiry meant a site visit and a quote, days later, for people who were only ever curious.

02The call we made

Put the maths on the page. One number in — your monthly electricity bill — and the page returns system size, payback period, twenty-five year savings and CO₂ avoided, instantly, before anyone asks for your phone number.

It sorts the audience for free. People who like the number fill the form. People who don't leave — and they were never a site visit worth making.

03What we shipped
  • Live savings calculator driven by a single bill slider
  • Interactive district map showing installation coverage
  • Chat helper that answers subsidy questions before the form
  • Blog and CMS the client updates without calling us
  • Separate landing pages per segment — home, housing society, commercial

01The problem

A staffing firm sells to two people who want opposite things. A factory needs forty workers by Monday. A welder needs a job. Both land on the same homepage, and every word written for one of them is noise to the other.

Worse, both used to arrive through the same contact form. A hiring manager's enquiry sat in a queue behind two hundred CVs.

02The call we made

Split the site down that line and commit to it. The visitor declares who they are within one screen, and from that point on the language, the proof and the call to action are all built for them.

Two enquiry paths, two destinations. An employer's request never queues behind a job application again.

03What we shipped
  • Dual-audience structure carried through every page
  • Separate enquiry routes for employers and job seekers
  • Service breakdown written in the client's own terms
  • Mobile-first — most job seekers arrive on a phone

01The problem

Before booking a blood test, people ask the same two questions: what will it cost, and can someone come to my home? Most clinic sites hide both behind "call us". Plenty of people don't call. They book with whichever app shows a price.

The front desk had the opposite problem. Callbacks began with "so, what did you need?", and every call took two calls.

02The call we made

Publish the price list. Every common test shows the clinic's price next to the usual rate, and the full-body checkups are priced as packages. Nobody has to ring to find out.

Then make booking two short steps. What you need first, your number second. There's no advance payment and no account. The callback starts with the test already known, and the report arrives on WhatsApp.

03What we shipped
  • Two-step booking: the service first, then name and mobile, with a spam trap
  • Public test prices and checkup packages shown next to the usual rates
  • Search by health concern: heart, liver, thyroid, diabetes and more
  • Doctor timetable with each specialist's OPD days and slots
  • Free home collection explained in four steps, from booking to report on WhatsApp
  • FAQ on fasting, report times and service areas, with click-to-call and WhatsApp everywhere
