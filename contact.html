Before you write

Six things people ask us first.

Answered here so you don't have to spend the call on them.

01 Do I need to know what I want before I message?
No, and pretending otherwise is how people end up buying the wrong thing. “Not sure yet” is a real option in the brief above. Tell us the problem — enquiries have dried up, the old site takes eight seconds to load, we're quoting by hand and it's eating the day — and working out what to build is our job, not yours.
02 Is the first call really free?
Yes, and so is the written scope note that follows it. Nothing is billed until you've seen a fixed price and a fixed date and said yes to both. If you take that scope note to another studio and build it with them, that's allowed — it's your document.
03 My budget is smaller than your pricing page. Should I still write?
Write. Every number on the pricing page is a starting point, not an invoice, and early-stage startups and first-time local businesses get flexible terms — part up front, the rest on launch. If the budget genuinely doesn't reach, we'll say so plainly and point you somewhere honest rather than quietly cutting quality to fit.
04 How soon can you actually start?
We run a small number of builds at once, on purpose — it's the only way you get the person writing the code answering your messages. That usually means a start inside a week or two. Tell us your deadline in the brief and we'll tell you straight whether it's real, before you commit anything.
05 I already have a site. Can you just fix it?
Sometimes. If it's hand-coded, usually yes. If it's a page builder held together by fourteen plugins, a rebuild is often cheaper than the surgery — and we'll show you the arithmetic rather than assert it. Send the link with your brief and you'll get an honest read on which one it is.
06 What happens to what I send you?
It arrives on one phone and one inbox. There is no form service in the middle, no CRM, no mailing list and no automated follow-up sequence. We keep the thread while there's a live conversation, and delete it on request — just ask.
